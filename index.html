Each leg of a parlay carries its own margin, and the margins multiply. Enter the legs from different matches to see the odds you are really getting.

What this can and cannot do

  • It cannot read Parimatch. Odds move, so type them in just before you bet.
  • Covering every result on one bookmaker's odds always loses the margin. A guaranteed profit only exists when two bookmakers price the same match differently, and those gaps close within minutes.
  • The outcome profile uses a Poisson goals model fitted to your odds. It treats the two teams' goals as independent and knows nothing about line-ups, cards or late news.
  • Goalscorer bets are left out because no split can cover them, and they usually carry a larger margin than the markets above.
  • Parlay legs from the same match are linked, so the chance shown for them is not reliable. Use legs from different matches.
